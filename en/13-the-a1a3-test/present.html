Do I need it?
Yes.
If you fly drones, get it. It’s 10 €, online, and one of the easiest tests you’ll ever take.
1
The law asks for it
For a drone of 250 g or more without a C0 label. And at HOBIzone: for flying FPV outdoors under the A3 rules.
2
Even when it doesn’t
C0 or under 250 g? Get it anyway. It’s honest and simple, and our club flies with it.
3
Under 16?
You can’t take the test yet. You fly next to a qualified pilot who supervises you. At HOBIzone, the coach.
Free to use in your own trainings. Just tell people where you got it.

Four steps, all online
Everything happens in LOIS, Transpordiamet’s online system: lois.transpordiamet.ee.
1
Study
Read the free study material on the Transpordiamet website. Nine short chapters, in Estonian.
2
Apply
Log in to LOIS and submit an application for the A1/A3 certificate.
3
Pay 10 €
The state fee, paid with a bank link right there in LOIS.
4
Take the test
Online, in LOIS, from home. 40 questions.
Free to use in your own trainings. Just tell people where you got it.

Think you know it all? Think again
Like a driving theory test: the right answer is the rule, not what feels logical.
the test has camera drones in mind
1
Written for camera drones
GPS, return-to-home, a phone screen. Much of it doesn’t match how we fly FPV. Learn it anyway.
2
Rules over experience
Good pilots fail by answering from their own flying. Read every question as the rulebook would.
Study the material as it is written, even the parts that feel obvious.
Free to use in your own trainings. Just tell people where you got it.

A certificate from any EASA country is valid in Estonia. You don’t have to take it here.

Luxembourg’s test, from your sofa
Luxembourg
Many Estonian pilots take it there: free and fully online, on Eurocontrol’s LearningZone.
1
Why people choose it
No fee, a clearer test site, and a choice of English, French, German or Luxembourgish.
2
Same certificate
Valid 5 years in Estonia and all EASA countries. You still register as an operator in Estonia.
Free to use in your own trainings. Just tell people where you got it.
