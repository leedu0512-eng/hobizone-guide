in one place.

This is the HOBIzone library: short talks with slides and an article, used by our trainers at practice and by families at home. We made it so the same things don’t have to be explained from scratch at every practice, and so a pilot can read again at home what was shown in the hall. We keep making it because every season brings new pilots, new drones and new questions, and every answer we write down helps the next group.
