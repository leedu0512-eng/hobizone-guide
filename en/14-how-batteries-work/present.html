A battery is a chemical pump
Chemistry inside pushes electrons out of one end and back in at the other. Three numbers tell you how.
1
Voltage (V)
How hard it pushes. More volts, faster motors.
2
Capacity (mAh)
How long it lasts. More mAh, longer flights, more weight.
3
Current (C)
How fast it can give it all out. Racing needs a lot, very fast.

Batteries all around you
Each type is good at something. None of these can feed a racing drone.
1
Alkaline AA · 1.5 V
TV remotes, clocks. Not rechargeable, and weak at high current.
2
NiMH AA · 1.2 V
The rechargeable AA, like in the Droonijalka remote. Safe, but heavy for its energy.
3
Car battery · 12 V
Lead-acid, 6 cells. Huge current to start an engine, but about 14 kg.
4
Phone · Li-ion
Light and full of energy, with a protection circuit. Gives its power slowly.
5
18650 / 21700 · 3.6 V
Round Li-ion cells. Lots of energy, used for long-range drones, not racing.
6
LiFePO4 · 3.2 V
A safer lithium type. Heavier for the same energy.

Why we fly LiPo
A LiPo is a lithium-ion battery in a soft pouch. Light, and it can give a huge current.
1
Light
Lots of energy for its weight. Every gram counts on a drone.
2
Huge current
A cell has only 1 to 4 mΩ of internal resistance. An AA has about 100 times more.
3
No protection
No circuit inside to stop it overcharging or emptying. Your charger and you protect it.
That last one is why the Battery safety talk exists.

The C rating, and why not to trust it
1C = the capacity in amps. A 1300 mAh pack at 1C gives 1.3 A.
1
What the label says
“100C” on a 1300 mAh pack would mean 130 A. Makers are known to overstate it.
2
A better health check
Internal resistance. Measure it when the pack is new and compare later.
3
Charging
Charge at 1C unless the label says more: 1.3 A for a 1300 mAh pack.

Charging and storing
A LiPo lives longest half full, cool and dry.
1
Balance charger
Charges every cell to the same voltage. Plug in the balance lead, always.
2
Right type
LiPo or LiHV, the right cell count, 1C. Check before you press start.
3
Storage voltage
Not flying in the next day or two? Charge or discharge to 3.8 V per cell.
4
Full packs age
A year full at room temperature keeps about 80 % of capacity. Half full: about 96 %.
5
Never below 0 °C
Charging a frozen pack damages it for good. Warm it to room temperature first.
6
Cool place
Store at 10 to 20 °C. Never in a hot car or in the sun.

Remember
Volts push, mAh last, C delivers.
1
We fly LiPo because it’s light and gives a huge current.
2
A cell: 4.2 V full, 3.8 V storage, land at 3.5 V, never below 3.0 V.
3
4S = four cells in a row. More cells, more volts.
4
Wh = volts × amp-hours. Airlines count Wh.
5
Don’t trust the C number. Charge at 1C with a balance charger.
6
Store at 3.8 V, cool. Never charge below 0 °C.
